GREENFIELD INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY - FEE STRUCTURE (ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-27)

BACHELOR OF COMPUTER ENGINEERING (4-YEAR PROGRAM)

Semester-wise Tuition Fee: NPR 85,000 per semester
Admission Fee (one-time, first semester only): NPR 25,000
Library Fee: NPR 2,000 per semester
Laboratory Fee: NPR 8,000 per semester
Examination Fee: NPR 3,500 per semester
Student Welfare Fund: NPR 1,000 per semester

Total for Semester 1: NPR 124,500
Total for Semester 2 onwards: NPR 99,500 per semester

IMPORTANT DEADLINES:
- Semester fee payment deadline: Within the first 15 days of each semester start date.
- Late payment fee: NPR 500 per week after the deadline, up to a maximum of 4 weeks.
- If fees remain unpaid after 4 weeks past the deadline, the student's registration may be cancelled and re-admission will require special approval from the Dean.

HOSTEL FEES (OPTIONAL):
- Double-sharing room: NPR 45,000 per semester (includes meals)
- Single-occupancy room: NPR 65,000 per semester (includes meals)
- Hostel fees are payable at the time of room allotment confirmation, separately from tuition.

SCHOLARSHIPS AND FEE WAIVERS:
- Merit Scholarship: Top 5 rank holders in each semester receive a 25% tuition fee waiver for the following semester.
- Need-based Financial Aid: Students can apply through the Scholarship Cell with income certificates; awards are decided case by case.
- Sports Quota Scholarship: Students representing the college at national-level sports events are eligible for a 15% fee waiver.

PAYMENT METHODS:
Fees can be paid via bank transfer to the college's official account, online payment through the student portal, or in person at the accounts office (cash or card). Cheques are not accepted.

REFUND POLICY:
- If a student withdraws within the first 7 days of semester start, 90% of the tuition fee is refundable.
- Withdrawal after 7 days but within 30 days: 50% refund.
- No refund after 30 days from semester start.
- Admission fee, library fee, and laboratory fee are non-refundable under all circumstances.
