GREENFIELD INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: What are the college office hours?
A: The administrative office is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Saturday 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. The office is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Q: How do I apply for a bonafide certificate?
A: Submit a request form (available at the admin office or student portal) along with your student ID. Certificates are processed within 3 working days and can be collected from the admin office.

Q: What is the process for hostel accommodation?
A: Students must submit the Hostel Application Form along with a copy of their admission letter and two passport-sized photographs. Rooms are allotted on a first-come-first-served basis, subject to availability. Hostel fees must be paid within 7 days of allotment confirmation.

Q: How can I get a duplicate ID card?
A: Report the loss to the security office first, then submit a Duplicate ID Request Form along with a fee of NPR 500 at the accounts office. The new card is issued within 5 working days.

Q: Who do I contact for scholarship-related queries?
A: Contact the Scholarship Cell located in Room 204, Administrative Block, or email scholarships@greenfieldtech.edu.np. Office hours: Monday to Friday, 10 AM to 4 PM.

Q: What is the attendance requirement to sit for exams?
A: Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible to appear for semester-end examinations. Students below 75% but above 65% may apply for condonation through the Dean's office.

Q: How do I request a transcript or migration certificate?
A: Fill out the Transcript Request Form available on the student portal, pay the applicable fee (NPR 800 for transcript, NPR 1000 for migration certificate) at the accounts office, and processing takes 10 working days.

Q: Is Wi-Fi available on campus?
A: Yes, campus-wide Wi-Fi is available to all enrolled students. Login credentials are provided during orientation week. For connectivity issues, contact the IT Help Desk in the Library building.

Q: How do I report ragging or harassment?
A: Contact the Anti-Ragging Committee immediately at antiragging@greenfieldtech.edu.np or call the 24-hour helpline at 01-4XXXXXX. All complaints are treated confidentially.

Q: Where can I find the academic calendar?
A: The academic calendar is published on the college website under "Academics" and is also displayed on notice boards in each department.
